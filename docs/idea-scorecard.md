# Organize Idea Scorecard — Week 1

Method: all 50 catalog entries read in full and independently triaged (not just the pre-ranked shortlist). Eight serious contenders were adversarially red-teamed (one dedicated skeptic each), then re-scored by a three-judge panel mirroring the official rubric (50% CLAD Execution / 25% UX / 25% Creativity & Usefulness). A white-space scan of all 50 concluded **no challenger concept is warranted** — the catalog covers ~15 distinct Organize concept families and avoids every crowded default; its one actionable gap is a *capability garnish* (no entry claims generated audio via build-sfx/build-music — cheap CLAD breadth for whichever concept wins). Scores are decision aids; precision is not claimed.
